Build 21 adds in-app reporting, blocking and moderation for messages. A screen recording from a physical iPhone is attached. Sandbox Apple Account purchases now unlock the workspace for the demo company and for any company you create (server change, same build).

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Field-service management for trade businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical): the office schedules jobs and manages customers, quotes and invoices; engineers run jobs on site. Not for children.

SIGN-IN AND SUBSCRIPTION (REQUIRED TO USE THE WORKSPACE)
A subscription is required. The demo company has no active subscription, so the plan picker opens first.
Option A - demo account: sign in with the Sign-In Information credentials (Owner; fictional customers, jobs, invoices and engineers). It has 3 active users, so choose Team or Business (Starter allows 1).
Option B - new company: tap New company on the sign-in screen, confirm your email, sign in. Any plan works.
Buy with your Sandbox Apple Account and the workspace opens. If not at once, tap Restore purchases on the same screen.
Delete an account: More > Delete account (in-app, permanent).

HOW TO REVIEW
1. After subscribing, the Dashboard shows today's work and money owed.
2. Jobs, Calendar and Customers tabs: open a job or customer for details and history.
3. More > Quotes, Invoices, Dispatch, Reports.
4. More > Subscription & Users: plans, prices, Restore purchases, Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy.
5. More > Messages: report and block (below).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT: REPORT, BLOCK, FILTER, MODERATE (NEW IN BUILD 21)
Messages are private between members of the same company. There is no public content.
- Report: in More > Messages open a conversation, press and hold another person's message, tap Report message, pick a reason, optionally block the sender. Customer texts in More > Communications too.
- Block: press and hold a message and tap Block, or the shield button. Blocked users' messages are hidden. Unblock in More > Safety & reports.
- Filter: messages containing objectionable language are rejected before sending.
- Moderation: More > Safety & reports. Owners and Admins can remove a message, pause messaging, suspend the account or close the report. Reports about an Owner go to Harbour South.
- Every report alerts Harbour South moderators by email; we act within 24 hours. Reporters stay anonymous.
To test: open the Okafor install conversation and press and hold a message from Luis Ortega.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase: accounts and data storage (name, email, company records).
- Stripe Connect (optional): the trade business connects its own Stripe account to take card payments from its customers for completed real-world work. Harbour South holds no customer funds. Not connected in the demo company.
- Twilio (optional): business texting to the trade's own customers from a dedicated US number. Not set up in the demo company.
- Resend: transactional email (invoices, moderation alerts).
- Apple: in-app purchase.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Works the same in all regions. Business texting numbers are US numbers.
