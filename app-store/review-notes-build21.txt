PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Harbour South FieldOps is field-service management software for trade businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical). The office schedules and dispatches jobs and manages customers, quotes and invoices; field engineers run their jobs on site. For business owners, office staff and engineers. Not for children.

SIGN-IN
Demo credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields (Owner account with a demo company of fictional customers, jobs, quotes, invoices and engineers; no setup needed). Create an account: New company on the sign-in screen. Delete an account: More > Delete account (in-app, permanent).

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Sign in. Dashboard shows today's work and money owed.
2. Jobs, Calendar and Customers tabs: open a job or customer for details and history.
3. More > Quotes, Invoices, Dispatch, Reports.
4. More > Subscription & Users: plans, prices, Restore purchases, Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy.
5. More > Messages: report and block (below).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT: REPORT, BLOCK, FILTER, MODERATE (NEW IN BUILD 21)
Messages are private between members of the same company. There is no public content.
- Report: in More > Messages open a conversation, press and hold a message from another person, tap Report message, pick a reason (harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, spam, illegal activity, self-harm, other), add optional details, optionally block the sender. Customer texts in More > Communications can be reported the same way.
- Block: press and hold a message and tap Block, or use the shield button in the conversation. Blocked users' messages are hidden (tap Show to view). Unblock there or in More > Safety & reports.
- Filter: messages containing objectionable language are rejected before sending.
- Moderation: More > Safety & reports lists your reports and blocked people. Owners and Admins can remove a message, pause a person's messaging, suspend the account, or close the report. Reports about an Owner go to Harbour South.
- Every report alerts Harbour South moderators by email. We act within 24 hours and remove offending content and users. Reporters stay anonymous.
To test: open the Okafor install conversation and press and hold a message from Luis Ortega.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase: accounts and data storage (name, email, company records).
- Stripe Connect (optional): the trade business connects its own Stripe account to take card payments from its customers for completed real-world work. Harbour South holds no customer funds. Not connected in the demo company.
- Twilio (optional): business texting to the trade's own customers from a dedicated US number. Not set up in the demo company.
- Resend: transactional email (invoices, moderation alerts).
- Apple: in-app purchase.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Works the same in all regions. Business texting numbers are US numbers.

REGULATED INDUSTRY
